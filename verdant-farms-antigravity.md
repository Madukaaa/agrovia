# VERDANT FARMS — Sustainable Agriculture Website Brief

## Project Overview

Create a modern, premium, environmentally focused website for a fictional sustainable agriculture company called **Verdant Farms**.

### Brand Name
**VERDANT FARMS**

### Tagline
**Growing food. Restoring nature.**

### Core Concept
Verdant Farms is a sustainable agriculture company focused on producing healthy food while protecting and restoring the natural environment.

The brand combines:

- Agriculture
- Sustainability
- Technology
- Nature
- Regenerative farming
- Environmental responsibility

The website should **not feel like an e-commerce store first**. It should feel like a **premium corporate sustainability and agriculture brand website**, with optional e-commerce functionality.

---

# 1. Brand Purpose

Verdant Farms uses environmentally responsible farming methods to produce healthy crops while:

- Improving soil health
- Reducing unnecessary water consumption
- Supporting biodiversity
- Using renewable energy
- Reducing agricultural waste
- Supporting farmers and agricultural communities
- Applying technology to improve farming decisions
- Reducing environmental impact

---

# 2. Website Goals

The website has three primary goals.

## 2.1 Introduce the Company
Explain who Verdant Farms is, what it believes in, and what makes its approach different.

## 2.2 Demonstrate Sustainable Practices
Show how the company approaches soil health, water management, biodiversity, energy, waste, and technology.

## 2.3 Showcase Agricultural Products
Present the crops and products grown by Verdant Farms in an elegant, editorial way.

The overall website type is:

> **Corporate + Sustainability + Agriculture + Optional E-commerce**

## 8.4 Sustainable Farming Process

> **From Soil to Harvest**

```text

↓

02
PLANT

↓

03
GROW

↓

04
HARVEST

↓

05
RESTORE
```

Clicking or hovering on each step reveals additional content.

### Step Content

#### 01 — Healthy Soil
Healthy crops begin beneath the surface. We use composting, crop rotation and natural soil management techniques to maintain fertile farmland.

#### 02 — Plant
Crops are selected and planted based on soil conditions, seasonality, climate, and long-term field health.

#### 03 — Grow
Smart irrigation, crop monitoring, responsible nutrient management, and biodiversity-friendly practices support healthy crop development.

#### 04 — Harvest
Crops are harvested at the appropriate stage while reducing unnecessary waste and handling losses.

#### 05 — Restore
Crop residues, composting, soil cover, rotation, and land-restoration practices help prepare the farm for future growing cycles.

# 17. Interaction and Motion System

Use subtle, premium motion.

## Recommended Interactions

### Animated Statistics
Numbers count upward when entering the viewport.

### Horizontal Scroll
Use for:

- Farming process
- Sustainability practices
- Products
- Impact stories

### Image Reveal
Large images reveal through masking or clipping while scrolling.

### Hover Cards
Cards expand, darken, reveal text, or shift imagery on hover.

### Parallax
Use only lightly for hero and full-width image sections.

### Scroll-linked Sections
Use sticky copy paired with changing imagery.

### Micro-interactions

- Arrow movement on CTA hover
- Underline transitions
- Button fill transitions
- Gentle image scale
- Tab content fade

Avoid excessive animation.

# 20. Responsive Behaviour

The design must be fully responsive.

## Desktop

- Large full-width editorial layouts
- Horizontal scroll experiences
- Split-screen sections
- Rich hover states

## Tablet

- Two-column layouts where possible
- Simplified horizontal interactions
- Reduced spacing

## Mobile

- Single-column layout
- Large touch targets
- Swipeable cards where useful
- Simplified interactive map
- Horizontal process becomes swipeable or stacked
- Preserve premium typography and whitespace
